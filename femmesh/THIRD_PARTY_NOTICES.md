any third-party project**. Everything was written from the published formulae and papers listed in
`docs/REFERENCES.md`, plus first-principles derivations noted in the module documentation.

far. When that happens (see the licence rules in the project handoff), this file gets a provenance
table (file, derived from, upstream file, licence), the upstream notice is reproduced here, and the
derived files get an SPDX header.
